2. What data is processed

This website has no user accounts, no forms and no comments. We do not collect personal data ourselves. The following technical data is nevertheless processed when you visit:

  • Server logs. Like any web server, the infrastructure serving this site records technical request data: your IP address, the time of the request, the requested address (URL) and your browser’s user agent. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running the service securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operation of the service.
  • Map clicks. When you click the map, the coordinates of the click and the selected year are sent to our API to look up which state the place belonged to. This information is not linked to your identity and is not stored by the application. Because it is part of the request address, it may appear in the server logs described above together with your IP address.

3. Cookies, tracking and third parties

This website does not use cookies, does not store anything in your browser’s local storage, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It loads no third-party resources (fonts, scripts, map tiles or embeds): everything, including the map tiles, is served from our own servers. Links to external sites, such as GitHub, lead to services with their own privacy policies that we do not control.

If advertising or analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated first, and consent will be requested before any cookies or trackers are set, where the law requires it.
