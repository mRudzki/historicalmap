About this website

“History of Europe on a map” is an educational website provided free of charge, with no advertising, no accounts and no cookies. Click a place in Europe to see which state or pre-state polity it belonged to, and how that changed over time.

About the author

This is a hobby project, built in my spare time out of curiosity about how the borders of Europe have moved over the centuries. If you enjoy it, have a look at my GitHub and LinkedIn.

I also run mediqcare.pl, a business website for doctors. That is the place for professional enquiries: you will find the business contact there, along with a more polished description of what I do.

Accuracy and liability

Historical borders are disputed and often uncertain, and the data used here is approximate and partly a work in progress. The website is provided “as is”, for information and education only. It is not a legal, academic or authoritative source; please verify important facts elsewhere.
