About this website

“History of Europe on a map” is a free, educational website. Click a place in Europe to see which state or pre-state polity it belonged to, and how that changed over time.

Accuracy and liability

Historical borders are disputed and often uncertain, and the data used here is approximate and a work in progress. The website is provided “as is”, for information and education only. It is not a legal, academic or authoritative source; please verify important facts elsewhere.
